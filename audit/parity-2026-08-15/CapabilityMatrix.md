**Audit date:** 2026-08-15 · **Commit:** `e15df56e3` (`compliance/dpdp`), working tree clean
**Compiled from:** `research/*.md` (competitor state), `inventory/*.md` (repo state),

## How to read this

**Status vocabulary** (this codebase's own classification, used throughout inventory and
gap files): `COMPLETE` · `PARTIAL` · `BROKEN` · `MOCKED` · `DEAD` · `MISSING` ·
`NOT_APPLICABLE` (architecturally inappropriate for that surface, not a gap) ·
`NEEDS_VALIDATION` (evidence points both ways or wasn't independently confirmed in this pass).

**Severity**, where a row cites a filed gap: `P0` blocks a primary workflow · `P1` major
parity gap · `P2` product-quality gap, works but below the benchmark · `P3` enhancement/
differentiation. Full detail for any `CAPS-ID` cited below is in
`gaps/domain-*.json`/`.md` and indexed in `GapMatrix.md`.

**"Stronger reference" is the point of this table.** It names whose design an implementer
should study for that specific row — sometimes a competitor, sometimes this product's own
better-built sibling surface (several gaps here are "X already solved this on mobile/web;
port it," not "go copy ChatGPT"), and in a meaningful minority of rows, neither competitor —
because this product is already ahead, or because the competitor's version is a documented
anti-pattern research explicitly flags as not worth copying.

**Corrections carried forward**, per the audit's own retraction record: this document
does **not** repeat two retracted findings from earlier drafts. (1) The "3 duplicate
sign-in/sign-up routes" finding was wrong — `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, `/register`,
`/auth/login` are documented single-purpose `redirect()` aliases with named real callers,
not duplication (`gaps/domain-shell-nav-ia.md` §1a, `gaps/domain-dead-code.md` §3). (2) The
"product is not publicly reachable" finding was wrong — `https://agiworkforce.com` is live,
public, and served with a strict CSP/HSTS posture (`inventory/deployment-state.md`); it is
_production's promotion pipeline_, not public reachability, that has an open gap (`GAP-P0-003`,
carried in `deployment-state.md`, not restated as a capability row here since it is a release
process fact, not a product-capability fact).
