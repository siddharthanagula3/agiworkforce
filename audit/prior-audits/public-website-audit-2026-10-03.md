## What the audit does not prove

It is a public-page browser audit. It did not read the repository, submit a
form, attempt a purchase or sign in, and it states that no critical outage,
security breach or legal noncompliance is established. Three facts are
therefore unverified against the running site and stay unverified here until
someone with authority checks them:

- TC03: whether the storage bucket is publicly addressable, and what it holds.
  The code and the published policy text were read, the bucket was not.
- FR09: the payments check was seen failing on 3 October 2026 at 18:51 GMT and
  was still failing at 23:05 UTC, when the production log gave the reason:
  `1 configured Stripe Price(s) are not active recurring Prices`. The products
  read before it succeeded, so the Stripe API answers. No checkout was
  attempted, so customer impact is unknown.
- FR05: the plan-first exception was read in code and in the pinned test, not
  observed in a live run.
