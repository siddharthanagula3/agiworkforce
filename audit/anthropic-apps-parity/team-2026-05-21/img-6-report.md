## Cross-surface UX signatures (TL;DR)

1. **Bottom-anchored composer with embedded model pill** is the single layout invariant across all 6 surfaces; the model selector is never a top-bar element.
2. **One permission vocabulary** — Always allow / Needs approval / Blocked / Custom (desktop tools) and Ask before acting / Act without asking (Chrome) — appears everywhere agentic behavior is exposed, always inline with the action.
3. **Three-tier model roster + extended-thinking toggle + identical "Opus consumes usage limits faster" warning** is repeated verbatim on desktop, mobile, and Chrome side-panel.
4. **Connectors and skills share a flyout grammar from the composer's `+` button**, and the connectors directory is a left-rail-of-verticals + right-grid-of-cards modal that scales identically from desktop to mobile.
5. **Reasoning and tool-call streams are always vertical, left-aligned, collapsible**, with a clock-icon header for thinking and a 1-line `>` chip on mobile that opens a full-height "Thought process" sheet.
