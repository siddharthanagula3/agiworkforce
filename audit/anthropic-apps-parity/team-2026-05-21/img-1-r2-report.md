Several hour estimates were too low for what they actually require (Code-mode dashboard, projects detail, Customize hub, research panel). One pattern — **Claude Design** as a sibling Anthropic Labs product surface — was missed entirely.

- I conflated two distinct connector-add flows (OAuth vs custom-MCP) into one row.

- Hour estimates skewed low on the heavyweight surfaces (Code empty-state dashboard, Customize hub, Project detail). They skewed slightly high on a handful of midweight surfaces (Add-menu, Research panel, Inline reasoning chip).
- Severity calls were mostly right. Three reclassifications, all low-stakes.
- Grand total of r1 hours: **806h**. Grand total of r2 hours (after adds + shifts): **~1,124h**, roughly **+40%**. The increase is driven mostly by Artifacts (+88h), Cowork sidebar route set (+24h), and underestimates on Code dashboard, Customize hub, Projects detail (+48h total).
