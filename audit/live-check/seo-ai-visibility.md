## Decision

Prioritize an accurate managed-web proposition and branded purchase answers, then a useful subscription buying guide. Do not acquire customers on a promise of a released local client, mobile app, extension or CLI. Do not claim that catalog membership means access on every managed plan.

The site has a functioning public HTML foundation. All 107 URLs in the live sitemap returned 200, self-canonical and indexable by their observed meta directives. An additional 46 help articles and 19 help collections returned 200 and self-canonical. The articles were absent from that sitemap. This is a discovery improvement, not proof they were unindexed: public search already returned some help pages.

In the sampled personalized Google and Bing results, `/pricing` was the first standard organic result for **AGI Workforce pricing**. AGI was absent from the inspected standard results for four unbranded queries. Search positions, brand mentions, recommendations and links are separate observations. No evidence in this audit establishes a ranking or conversion improvement from the local changes.
