## Do not misclassify these as defects

- A historical answer's model differing from the currently selected next-turn model is expected. S3/S4 do not establish a model-routing error.
- Empty space above a bottom-anchored composer in a short conversation is not, by itself, a defect.
- Ellipsis on a long sidebar title is not, by itself, a defect. Verify tooltip/full-title access, keyboard behavior and available width.
- A missing user photo is not proven by the fallback avatar. The screenshots do not establish that a profile photo exists.
- Local/Cloud boundary disclosure has a security purpose. Simplifying visual controls must not hide an active route or permit silent cross-boundary routing.
- Claude's blank window is a reference-access problem, not a layout to imitate or evidence of its intended design.
