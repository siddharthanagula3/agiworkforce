Hybrid Working Policy (revision 4, effective 2026-07-01)

1. Scope. This policy applies to all full-time employees outside the retail stores.

2. Remote days. Employees may work remotely for up to three days in each calendar
week. Remote days do not carry over from one week to the next.

3. Core hours. Everyone must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local time on
working days, whether in the office or remote.

4. Equipment. The company provides one monitor and one headset for home use. Other
equipment requires manager approval.

5. Exceptions. Longer remote arrangements need written approval from both the
employee's manager and the People team, and are reviewed every six months.
