03 · How we use it, and on what basis One row per purpose, rather than a sentence listing four bases and leaving you to work out which applies to what. Where a row says legitimate interests, it also says why we think ours do not override yours. That balancing test is the part a bare list omits. Creating and running your account Data used: email, account identifier, authentication metadata, settings. Basis: performance of a contract. You asked us to run an account; it cannot exist without these. Running the assistant (Managed Cloud) Data used: conversations, files, projects, memories, schedules. Basis: performance of a contract. This is the service itself. Local and BYOK do not produce this data for us at all. Taking payment Data used: billing identifiers, plan, invoice metadata. Card numbers go to Stripe and never reach us. Basis: performance of a contract, and legal obligation for the records tax and accounting law requires us to keep. Answering you Data used: support conversations, feedback, content reports. Basis: performance of a contract when you are a customer; legitimate interests otherwise. You initiated the contact, and we cannot reply without keeping what you sent. Keeping the service secure and available Data used: server logs, server and edge operational error reports, the security audit log, rate-limiting state, account status. Basis: legitimate interests. Every user has an interest in the service not being taken over or abused, the data is operational rather than content, and you cannot opt out of it without also opting out of being protected by it. Preventing referral abuse Data used: the referral program checks described above. Basis: legitimate interests. Referral rewards are meant for genuine new customers, and the checks compare signals you already give us or Stripe already holds instead of adding new tracking. Understanding which builds are downloaded Data used: download records: hashed IP, user-agent, referrer, coarse country, plus the raw IP in server logs, as section 01 says. Basis: legitimate interests. Narrow. The caveats about the fixed salt and the raw log entry are in section 01. Crash and error reporting Data used: consented browser error reports with a stable user id. Sensitive request fields are removed and free-text diagnostics are masked for secret-shaped values. Basis: your consent. Off unless you turn it on in settings. Server and edge operational error reporting falls under Keeping the service secure and available above and does not attach a stable user id. Product analytics Data used: aggregated page views on this site, and product usage events from the AGI apps on web, desktop, mobile, Chrome, VS Code and the command line, recorded against your account: that a response was stopped or regenerated, that a suggested edit was accepted or dismissed, and similar product steps, with fixed labels such as your plan or the model provider. They never carry your messages, code, files or file names. Basis: your consent. Nothing loads or is recorded until you give it, and the gate fails closed: if your choice cannot be read, analytics stays off. Analytics consent given before this disclosure (notice revision product-analytics-v1) covered page views only, so the app events need you to allow analytics again. Change it any time from Settings in any AGI app or on the data rights page; a workspace administrator can also turn it off for every member. Telling you when something opens Data used: the email you gave the early-access list. Basis: your consent, recorded per purpose against the revision of this notice you were shown, before the address is stored. Marketing email Data used: your account email. Purpose: Email me product news, tips and offers. Basis: your consent, asked in a separate unticked box at sign-up or when you first accept the terms after signing in, if no decision is on record for this purpose. You can also choose in Settings, Privacy, or withdraw on the [/privacy/requests] data rights page. Your decision is recorded against the revision of the privacy notice you were shown. No marketing email is sent by the product yet. Complying with the law Data used: whatever a valid legal process compels, narrowed to the minimum. Basis: legal obligation. India works differently and has its own page. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 consent is the default ground rather than one of several, so the analysis is not the same as the table above. It is at [/privacy/india] /privacy/india, and it governs for data principals in India where the two differ.
