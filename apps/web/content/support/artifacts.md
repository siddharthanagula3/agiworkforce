## What an artifact is

When an answer produces a document, a piece of code or a rendered page rather
than a paragraph, it opens as an artifact in a panel beside the conversation.
The panel can be opened and closed from the conversation, or with
Shift+Cmd/Ctrl+A, and has a fullscreen view.

## Editing one

Edits you make in the panel are saved as new versions, and earlier versions can
be restored. **Duplicate** makes an independent copy, confirmed with
"Duplicated. Edits here no longer touch the original." **Save to project** files
it into a project's knowledge.

If someone else changed the same artifact first, the panel keeps your work and
says so: their version is shown and your edit is kept as the latest version.
Nothing is silently discarded.

## Getting a copy out

**Copy artifact** puts the source on the clipboard. **Download file** and
**Download image** save it. From the Library, an artifact exports as PDF or
Word.

## Publishing

Publishing an artifact gives it a link. The audience works like a shared
conversation: anyone with the link, or your workspace only, and the panel
confirms which is in force. Changing it later asks first ("Make this public
again?", "Limit this to your workspace?").

Settings holds a **Published artifacts** list with **Unpublish artifact** on
each one. Unpublishing stops the link working.

## When a preview will not render

"Inline preview unavailable" or "Image preview unavailable" means the panel
cannot render that content in place, not that the artifact is damaged. Download
it and open it locally.
