## Create the account

1. Open the sign-up page and enter your age in **Your age**.
2. Choose **Continue with Google** or **Continue with GitHub**, or enter the
   email address you want the account under and press **Continue**.
3. If you used email, enter the code sent to that address. If the code has not
   arrived, check the spam folder before requesting another.
4. If a **Create a password** screen follows, choose the password you will use
   with that address to log in.

The sign-up form asks for your age in a field labelled "Your age". You must be
at least 13 to create an account, and if you are under 18 you need permission
from a parent or guardian. The age is checked in your browser and is not stored
or sent to us. There is no terms checkbox: under the button the form states "By
creating an account, you agree to the Terms of Use and acknowledge the Privacy
Policy." One optional box, "Email me product news, tips and offers.", starts
unticked.

When a provider button on the sign-in page, such as **Continue with Google**,
creates a new account, the account's first screen asks for your age and for you
to accept the terms, and the account cannot be used until the terms are ticked
and an eligible age is entered. The code always comes before the password: the
sign-up page asks for a password only after the code proves the address is
yours. The password screen states its rule before you type: use at least 8
characters, and a password that is easy to guess or found in a data breach is
refused.

## What you can do immediately

The {{PLAN.free}} plan is open by default. A new account can open the chat surface and
send a message straight away: it has no waitlist and no invite code to redeem.
A self-serve paid plan starts at checkout when paid upgrades are open to your
account; while they are not, it needs an access code, and without one you join
the upgrade waitlist.

## Send the first message

Open the chat surface, type into the composer, and press Enter. The composer
placeholder reads "Ask anything. Type / for commands". Below the composer the
model selector shows which model will answer; leaving it on **Auto** lets AGI
pick a model for each message from the ones your plan and trust mode allow.

## If sign-up does not complete

- "No account uses this email. Sign up instead." on a sign-in screen means the
  address has never been registered. Sign up rather than resetting a password.
- "This email already has an account. Log in instead." on the sign-up screen
  means the address is registered already. Log in with it rather than signing
  up again.
- A refused password keeps you on the password screen with the reason under the
  field. A password refused as too weak also lists suggestions for a stronger
  one.
- A sign-up that needs a detail the screen cannot collect ends with a message
  that starts "This sign-up also needs" and tells you to contact support. Email
  {{CONTACT.email}} to finish creating the account.
