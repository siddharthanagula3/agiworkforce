## Create a share link

Open the conversation's menu and choose **Share conversation**. Two audiences
are offered:

- **Anyone with the link**, described as best for a quick review.
- **Share with workspace**, for longer collaboration, which limits the link to
  members of your workspace.

Once the link exists the dialog confirms the audience: "Anyone with the link can
open this now", or "Only your workspace can open this now". Copy it from the
dialog, or open the link to check what a reader sees.

## Change or revoke a link

Switching the audience after the fact asks first, naming the change: "Make this
readable by anyone with the link?" or "Limit this to your workspace?".
**Revoke share** removes access, and the confirmation says that anyone using the
link will immediately lose access.

## Find every link you have created

Settings, **Shared links** lists the links created from web conversations, with
the ones marked _Workspace only_ distinguished from public ones, and revokes any
of them. Published artifacts have their own list in Settings with an
**Unpublish artifact** action.

## Export instead of sharing

The conversation menu also exports. From the Library, an artifact exports as PDF
or Word. There is no spreadsheet export on web, so no xlsx option is offered
rather than one that would fail after you picked it.

## Chats you cannot share

A temporary chat cannot be shared while the control is on, because it is never
saved. Turn temporary chat off first.
