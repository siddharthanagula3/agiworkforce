## Self-service export

In Settings, Privacy, choose **Export data** to request an account data export.
After the request is accepted, the page shows a preparation notice. A request
that fails shows an error.

## What else you can ask for

The privacy requests page is the formal rights path: review your consent record,
or ask AGI to access, correct or erase your data. Settings links to it directly
so you do not have to find it from a policy page.

## Files and generated media

In Settings, Privacy, choose **Manage** beside **Uploaded files** to open the
Library, where you can review or delete individual items. Uploads and generated
media are covered by the same rights path as conversations.

## Before you delete your account

Export first. See the account deletion article for the timing and the
cancellation window.
