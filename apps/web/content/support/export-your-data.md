## Self-service export

Settings, Privacy carries **Download all your conversations as JSON**. The
export runs in the browser and downloads a single file named for the day you
ran it. It is a self-service download, not a request you wait on.

## What else you can ask for

The privacy requests page is the formal rights path: review your consent record,
or ask AGI to access, correct or erase your data. Settings links to it directly
so you do not have to find it from a policy page.

## Files and generated media

Uploads and generated media are listed in your Library, and in Settings under
the data section, where individual items can be reviewed or deleted. They are
covered by the same rights path as conversations.

## Before you delete your account

Export first. Account deletion erases conversations and account data, and the
export cannot be run afterwards. See the account deletion article for the
timing and the cancellation window.
