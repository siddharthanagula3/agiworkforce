## If you are in danger now

AGI is software. It is not a crisis service, and it cannot call for help or send
anyone to you. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call your
local emergency number now.

## Who to call

**United States.** Call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline,
or chat at [chat.988lifeline.org](https://chat.988lifeline.org/). The service
describes itself as free, confidential and open at all hours. To text in
Spanish, send AYUDA to 988.

**India.** Call Tele MANAS on 14416, or on 1-800-891-4416. It is a toll-free
helpline of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, open at all hours, and
you choose your language when you call.

**Anywhere else.** [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com/) lists free
helplines by country. The International Association for Suicide Prevention
points to the same directory. If you cannot find a line for your country, call
your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.

We checked these numbers against the services' own pages on 7 October 2026.
Numbers change. If one does not connect, use the service's own website.

## What AGI does when a message is about self-harm

Nothing special, and you should know that before you rely on it.

- AGI has no feature that detects a message about suicide or self-harm.
- A chat shows no crisis banner, helpline number or pop-up.
- A message about self-harm alerts no person at AGI.
- The model you picked answers under its provider's own safety rules. What it
  says, and whether it mentions a helpline, differs from one model to another.

One setting touches this subject. **Reduce sensitive content**, in Settings under
Safety, is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, AGI refuses a short list of
explicit or harmful how-to prompts before they reach a Managed Cloud model, and
requests for suicide or self-harm methods are on that list. The refusal says the
content is unavailable while the setting is on. It shows no helpline.

## What the rules say

The [acceptable use policy](https://agiworkforce.com/acceptable-use) does not
allow using AGI to encourage, instruct or glorify suicide, self-harm or
disordered eating. Asking for help and talking about how you feel are allowed.

## Not medical advice

Nothing a model writes is medical or mental-health advice. The
[disclaimer](https://agiworkforce.com/disclaimer) explains the limits of model
output. For a decision about your health, talk to a qualified professional.
