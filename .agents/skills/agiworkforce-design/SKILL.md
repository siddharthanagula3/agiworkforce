# AGI Workforce design

Use this skill first for any visible interface work; it outranks the generic design skills and tells you which of them to load at each stage.

1. Start from the product definition: AGI Workforce is the ChatGPT and Claude apps, feature for feature and feel for feel, with two differences only, any model and any API provider. When the two leaders agree, do it their way; when they differ, take the clearer one; remove what exists only to look different; add only where a model or provider is chosen, routed, shown or billed. Then name the surface before designing: product UI (chat, projects, settings, code, artifacts) or public page (landing, features, pricing, surface pages, legal). The two have different targets and different reference material.
2. Product UI target: a ChatGPT and Claude user must feel at home. Follow the shared convention recorded in docs/research/leader-ui-measurements-2026-09-04.md section 9 and the priorities in docs/research/chat-parity-gap-matrix-2026-09-04.md: sidebar 260 wide with 34px rows that are links, one centred column of 768, a 48 header, a one-row composer at 52 with a ring and no permanent shadow, user bubbles at most 75 percent wide with radius 18, assistant prose 16px on 26px with no container, a hover-gated action row of five to seven icons, model picker rows of 48 with one guidance line, progress inside the assistant turn that collapses to one line when done, Stop in the send slot. Capabilities from the model registry decide controls, never a provider name.
3. Public page target: product-led, calm, precise. One public design system, the 28 August flagship system (commit dce4a8d6f) that the founder confirmed on 2026-09-11 with screenshots of the 29 August production pages: Newsreader for display, Geist for body, JetBrains Mono for receipts, eyebrows, ledger labels and terminals, warm black ground with cream ink and one amber accent in dark, warm paper ground with brown ink and a deepened amber in light, cream primary buttons that draw the ground colour as their label, and the route receipt as the signature motif. Every hero headline carries one italic amber fragment (agi-fl-h1-em on surface pages, the em prop of PageHero elsewhere), a mono fact ribbon sits under the hero, CSS device mockups from DeviceMockups render the product in the hero, and the closing section is the glow-lit FinalCta from SurfaceSections or the centred agi-lp-close. Motion stays restrained (Reveal, MotionReveal, Stage, ScrollDeck; no parallax, no tilt, no ticker outside the landing). The surface and product pages (desktop, mobile, cli, chrome-extension, vscode-extension, agi-code, business, docs, web) are built on SurfaceSections and LandingSections with their rules in features/marketing/components/legacy-pages.css; the landing keeps FlagshipSections and legacy-landing.css. Every claim, count, price, model name and surface status comes from the catalog, BILLING_PLAN_PRICING and SURFACE_STATUS.
4. Colour and type come only from the owners: packages/ui/design-tokens/src/foundation.css (primitives and semantic roles), packages/ui/design-tokens/src/chat.css (the --chat-\* contract, light and dark), the [data-design='agi'] blocks in apps/web/app/globals.css (one base block for the dark public system and one [data-theme='light'] override, both carrying the same token names), and next/font variables in apps/web/app/layout.tsx (Geist, Geist Mono, Newsreader, JetBrains Mono). A token names a role (fill, text, on-fill), not a hue; read .claude/rules/ui-colour-and-interaction.md before touching a colour and add a case to apps/web/shared/components/**tests**/theme-contrast.test.ts for any new token.
5. Use the existing primitives: useConfirmAction for anything irreversible, useMenuKeyboard for every role=menu panel, Spinner for loading, ProductFrame for screenshots (with a sizes prop that matches the rendered width), MotionReveal for section reveals. Both themes, keyboard and focus rings, 44px touch targets on coarse pointers, prefers-reduced-motion honoured, no horizontal overflow at 320.
6. Never ship the generic look: gradient blobs, aurora or glass, glow, floating logos or icons, decorative code, numbered section eyebrows, three equal feature cards, cards around prose, fake metrics or testimonials, repeated centred CTAs, oversized serif hero copy on product UI, italic accent fragments, solid red stop buttons, always-visible action rows.
7. Pipeline for new or substantially changed UI, in order: extract-design-system when the brief names a reference site; agiworkforce-design (this skill) plus frontend-design for the direction, design-taste-frontend only on public pages (redesign-existing-projects when reworking a shipped page); implement; the antislop family (antislop, antislop-ui, antislop-layoutmobile, antislop-copywriting, antislop-code) as the gate; web-design-guidelines and ms-frontend-design-review for interaction, accessibility and design-system compliance, with vercel-react-best-practices and vercel-composition-patterns on the code; fix; emil-design-eng, review-animations for motion and detail; then the browser comparison.
8. Browser comparison is the acceptance test, not a report: screenshot the real page at 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 in both themes with a script that scrolls first so lazy images load, measure with the probe recorded in docs/research/leader-ui-measurements-2026-09-04.md, and run the relevant specs (apps/web/e2e/marketing-stage-contrast.spec.ts for public pages; chat-layout and the transcript specs for product UI).
9. Guards that must pass before a commit: node scripts/check-css-tokens.mjs, node scripts/check-em-dash-in-copy.mjs, node scripts/check-no-hardcoded-model-ids.mjs, node scripts/check-theme-only-text-colours.mjs, the web typecheck, eslint and prettier; no explanatory comments, no hardcoded literals, lowercase commit subjects, no attribution trailers.

Do not invent a second design system or a one-off palette for a page; when the brief wants something the tokens cannot express, extend the token owner and its contrast test, then use it.
