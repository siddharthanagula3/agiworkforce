# AI SEO

Use this skill for AI search visibility, generative-engine optimization, and content extractability work.

## Workflow

1. Check for product, audience, and positioning context before recommending changes.
2. Organize findings around structure, authority, and presence.
3. Review whether pages answer target questions directly with clear headings, definitions, comparison tables, statistics, and citations.
4. Check whether AI crawlers and search bots are intentionally allowed or blocked.
5. Produce a prioritized action plan with measurable visibility checks.

## Boundaries

- Traditional crawl, indexation, schema, and traffic-loss audits belong to a technical SEO skill.
- Do not invent platform citation rates or crawler behavior. Verify current claims before using them in public docs.
