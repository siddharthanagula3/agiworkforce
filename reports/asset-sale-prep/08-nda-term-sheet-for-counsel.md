# NDA Term Sheet For Counsel

Status: Draft
Owner: Founder
Created: 2026-05-31

## Notice

This is not a legal contract and not legal advice. It is a business-terms checklist for an attorney to draft or review an NDA.

## Parties

- Disclosing party: founder personally and/or AGI Automation LLC / relevant selling entity.
- Receiving party: buyer entity and approved representatives.

## Purpose

Evaluate a potential transaction involving AGI Workforce assets, including source code, documentation, product strategy, architecture, brand/domain assets, package/release assets, and related IP.

## Confidential Information

Should include:

- Source code and repo access.
- Technical architecture.
- Product plans.
- Diligence materials.
- Demo videos not publicly released.
- Commercial and buyer-process materials.
- IP provenance and legal disclosures.
- Any non-public metrics, ad results, waitlist data, or customer/user feedback.

Should exclude:

- Publicly available information.
- Information independently developed by receiving party without use of confidential information.
- Information already known by receiving party before disclosure.
- Information rightfully received from another source.

## Non-Use

Receiving party may use confidential information only to evaluate the potential transaction.

## Non-Disclosure

Receiving party may disclose only to representatives who need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

## Source Code Access

Recommended restrictions:

- No source code before NDA.
- No cloning or copying except as expressly permitted.
- No training models on the code/docs.
- No redistribution.
- No production use.
- Access logs retained where possible.
- Source access limited to named diligence reviewers.

## AI Training Restriction

Ask counsel to include a clause prohibiting use of confidential materials to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or improve AI models, except with explicit written permission.

## Residuals

Avoid broad residuals language that lets buyer use remembered ideas from diligence. If buyer requires residuals, have counsel narrow it sharply.

## Term

Suggested:

- 2-3 years confidentiality for general business information.
- Trade secrets protected as long as they remain trade secrets.

## Return / Destruction

Upon request or process end:

- Destroy or return confidential materials.
- Remove repo access.
- Certify destruction if requested.
- Allow archival legal/compliance copies only if required.

## No Obligation

NDA does not require either party to proceed with a transaction.

## No License

Disclosure grants no license, ownership, or usage rights.

## Remedies

Injunctive relief for breach should be available.

## Governing Law

Counsel to choose based on selling entity and transaction posture.

## Founder Red Lines

- No source before NDA.
- No AI training on repo/docs.
- No broad residuals.
- No right to use product ideas without a signed transaction.
- No public announcement without written consent.
