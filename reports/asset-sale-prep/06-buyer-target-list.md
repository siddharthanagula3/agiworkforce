## Outreach Thesis

Do not sell this as "a repo with many files." Sell it as acceleration for a buyer already building one of these:

- AI coding agents.
- Cross-surface AI applications.
- Local/private AI.
- BYOK and multi-provider routing.
- Browser/desktop/IDE AI workflows.
- Artifact, connector, and model-selection infrastructure.

## Outreach Order

1. Warm intros to 20 strategic/operator contacts.
2. Direct outreach to 50 product/corp-dev leaders.
3. Developer-tool companies and local/privacy companies.
4. Marketplace listing only after strategic outreach has been attempted.

## Buyer Qualification Questions

Ask before sending confidential material:

- Are you interested in asset purchase, licensing, acquisition, or hiring?
- What part of the asset is most relevant: mobile, desktop, CLI, IDE, browser, model routing, docs?
- Do you have budget or authority for an asset acquisition?
- What diligence timeline would you expect?
- Would you want founder transition support?
