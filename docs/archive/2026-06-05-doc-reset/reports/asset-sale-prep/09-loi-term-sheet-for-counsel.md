# LOI Term Sheet For Counsel

Status: Draft
Owner: Founder
Created: 2026-05-31

## Notice

This is not a legal contract and not legal advice. It is a business-terms checklist for an attorney to draft or review a letter of intent or asset purchase term sheet.

## Transaction Type

Potential structures:

- Asset purchase.
- Entity acquisition.
- Exclusive license with purchase option.
- Asset purchase plus transition services.

Founder preference:

- Cash-heavy asset purchase.
- Clear closing timeline.
- Narrow reps/warranties.
- Separately compensated transition support.

## Purchased Assets

Potential included assets:

- Source code repository.
- Product documentation.
- Architecture docs.
- Build scripts and release workflows.
- Website/domain assets, if assignable.
- Brand assets, if assignable.
- Package names, marketplace listings, and app-store assets, if transferable.
- Demo videos and buyer-facing product collateral.
- Copyrights and other owned IP in the code/docs.

Potential excluded assets:

- Founder personal accounts.
- API keys, secrets, credentials.
- Competitive reference archives/screenshots unless explicitly included.
- Personal emails and private communications.
- Attorney-client privileged materials.
- Third-party materials not owned by seller.
- Pre-existing tools/subscriptions that cannot be assigned.

## Purchase Price

Potential structures:

- All cash at close.
- Cash at close plus milestone payment.
- Cash plus consulting/transition-services agreement.
- License fee plus purchase option.

Suggested founder target ranges to discuss with counsel/advisor:

- Strategic AI lab: open high, e.g. $7.5M-$12M ask.
- Developer-tool or privacy/open-model strategic: $1.5M-$5M ask.
- Lower-probability fast asset sale: $150k-$500k range.

Do not include these numbers in the first buyer email. Use them for negotiation planning.

## Escrow / Holdback

Founder preference:

- Minimize holdback.
- If required, limit amount and duration.
- Tie holdback only to specific reps/warranties, not broad product success.

## Reps And Warranties

Likely buyer asks:

- Seller owns or has rights to transfer assets.
- No known infringement except disclosed.
- Third-party license obligations disclosed.
- No hidden security incidents/secrets/customer data.
- No liens/encumbrances.

Founder should disclose exceptions rather than over-warrant.

## Conditions To Closing

Possible conditions:

- Satisfactory technical diligence.
- IP assignment completed.
- License disclosure accepted.
- Asset schedule finalized.
- Source code delivered.
- Domain/account transfer initiated.
- No material undisclosed liabilities.

## Exclusivity

Founder preference:

- Avoid exclusivity until buyer has given a real price range and timeline.
- If exclusivity is required, keep it short: 14-30 days.
- Include buyer diligence milestones and automatic expiration.

## Confidentiality And Announcement

- No announcement without mutual written consent.
- Founder may describe transaction only after close and subject to agreed wording.

## Transition Services

Possible:

- 30 days: handoff and setup.
- 60 days: integration support.
- 90 days: productization support.

Transition should be separately scoped and compensated if intensive.

## Closing Deliverables

- Source export or repo transfer.
- Build/run docs.
- License disclosure.
- IP assignment documents.
- Asset schedule.
- Domain/package/account transfer steps.
- Transition-services plan.

## Founder Red Lines

- No indefinite exclusivity.
- No source transfer before payment/closing.
- No broad warranty that the product is complete or revenue-generating.
- No obligation to work full-time post-close unless separately agreed.
- No buyer use of reference archives as owned IP.
