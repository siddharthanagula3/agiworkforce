Status: Draft

## Rules

- Keep the first email short.
- Do not attach source code.
- Do not overclaim traction.
- Do not say "I don't know how to code" in cold outreach.
- Say "AI-assisted development workflow" if asked about build process.
- Use a demo video and teaser before asking for an NDA.

## Cold Email: Strategic Buyer

Subject: AGI Workforce - local-first multi-surface AI app suite

Hi {{name}},

I’m the founder of AGI Workforce. Over the last 10 months I built a proprietary local-first, BYOK, multi-provider AI application suite across mobile, web, desktop, CLI, Chrome, and VS Code.

The strongest fit is for teams working on coding agents, local/private AI, BYOK, multi-provider routing, desktop/browser/IDE workflows, or AI application suites.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call next week? I can send a short non-confidential teaser and demo video first.

Siddhartha

## Ghosting Operating Rule

Track every target in a spreadsheet:

- Company
- Contact
- Role
- Source
- Date sent
- Follow-up 1
- Follow-up 2
- Response
- Fit score
- Notes
- Next action

Expected outcomes from 100 targeted messages:

- 60-80 no reply
- 10-20 polite no
- 5-10 calls
- 2-3 serious conversations
- 0-1 LOI

This is normal. Do not interpret ghosting as a personal verdict.
