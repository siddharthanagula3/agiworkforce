# Transition Services Outline

Status: Draft
Owner: Founder
Created: 2026-05-31

## Purpose

Define founder support after a sale so the buyer can absorb the asset without turning transition into unlimited unpaid work.

## Option A: 30-Day Handoff

Best for: buyer has strong internal engineering team and wants asset transfer.

Scope:

- Repo walkthrough.
- Build and verification setup.
- Product architecture review.
- Diligence Q&A.
- Known-risk walkthrough.
- Transfer of docs, domains/packages/accounts where applicable.

Founder availability:

- Up to 20 hours total.
- Async responses within 2 business days.
- 2 live walkthrough calls.

## Option B: 60-Day Integration Support

Best for: buyer wants to integrate selected parts into existing product.

Scope:

- Everything in Option A.
- Weekly technical Q&A.
- Integration mapping.
- Help identify reusable packages/crates.
- Help separate included/excluded assets.
- Support internal engineering handoff.

Founder availability:

- Up to 50 hours total.
- Weekly 60-minute call.
- Async responses within 1-2 business days.

## Option C: 90-Day Productization Support

Best for: buyer wants founder involved in packaging or shipping a derivative product.

Scope:

- Everything in Option B.
- Demo hardening support.
- Roadmap prioritization.
- Buyer team onboarding.
- Limited product strategy.
- Limited code review or AI-assisted implementation support.

Founder availability:

- Up to 100 hours total.
- Requires separate paid consulting or employment agreement.

## Out Of Scope Unless Separately Paid

- Full-time employment.
- Unlimited bug fixing.
- Support for buyer's unrelated codebase.
- App-store submission responsibility.
- Customer support.
- Legal/compliance warranties.
- Managed-cloud operations.
- New feature development beyond agreed transition tasks.

## Recommended Commercial Terms

Ask counsel/advisor to structure one of:

- Fixed transition fee.
- Hourly consulting rate.
- Included limited hours with overage rate.
- Employment/contractor agreement if buyer wants deeper involvement.

Founder goal: keep sale cash separate from transition labor.
