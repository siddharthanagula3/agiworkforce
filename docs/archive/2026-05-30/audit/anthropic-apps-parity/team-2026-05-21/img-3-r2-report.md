## Image-side observations the lead should reconcile

1. **No bottom tab bar** anywhere in 27 PNGs. Surface switching is exclusively via the left drawer (PNG 03). If our mobile has a bottom tab bar, that's a deliberate divergence that needs sign-off.
2. **Dispatch surface** (PNG 03, "New" pill) — not represented by any other PNG in the 27, so its UI is unknown to this audit. Likely related to Cowork (PNG 09) — mobile-driven desktop control.
3. **Health Beta** appears twice: in the Add-to-Chat sheet (PNG 27) and in Permissions (PNG 16, "Never"). HealthKit integration is the most platform-specific feature observed.
4. **Composer voice has TWO buttons** (mic + waveform-circle) — functionally distinct (dictation vs full voice-mode). Easy to under-spec as one button.
5. **Reasoning UI is two-layer** — inline collapsed chip in chat (PNG 24) + expandable bottom sheet with peek/full snap points (PNGs 25-26). Both layers need to exist or we lose the ambient awareness vs deep-dive distinction.
6. **Code surface is full-featured on mobile** — sessions list (PNG 08), session detail with monospace blocks (PNG 21), Plan/Code mode picker (PNG 20), Copy-branch overflow menu (PNG 21), photo-attach action sheet (PNG 22), archived list (PNG 23). This is a substantial mobile build, not a "view-only mirror".
7. **StoreKit IAP confirmed** — Restore Purchases row (PNG 17) is the App Store Review-mandated affordance. No external billing link, no price display — fully Apple-managed.
8. **Settings IA is 11 rows deep** (PNG 10). Two row types: drill-down (chevron-right) and inline-control (toggle or chevron-up/down for enum like Dark/Light).
9. **Permissions screen** (PNG 16) is the biggest mobile-native parity surface — iOS App Intents + EventKit + HealthKit + Core Location all in one settings drill-down. Heavy implementation; recommend lead size carefully against our current iOS native-access state.
10. **Time-of-day greeting** ("How can I help you this evening?" — PNG 01/02) varies by clock. Trivial detail but a polish signal.
