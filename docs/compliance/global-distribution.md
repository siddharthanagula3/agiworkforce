# Global distribution: obligations, evidence and gaps

Status: Draft applicability and evidence map; not approved for publication
Owner: Legal/compliance
Last reviewed: 2026-10-05, America/Chicago

This is the canonical working map for the public-site mission. It records current
repository evidence and regional questions from the [dated research
ledger](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md). It does not
certify global compliance, authorize a country offer, change the age rule, or
publish policy words. Legal/compliance and the owner decide applicability; the
lead owns enforcement, provider/consent configuration and publication.

## Evidence and scope

An obligation mentioned below is a **candidate requirement if its stated
jurisdiction, audience, processing, product, threshold and effective-date tests
apply**. Original region findings were verified by their workers on October 4;
only the ledger's `FRESH` propositions were independently refreshed October 5.
Every region row points to primary-source citations through stable research IDs.
Unknown, disputed, archived, draft and proposed findings stay in those states.
No regional requirement is inferred merely because a competitor publishes a
page or because AGI has a translated page, payment currency or provider.

The map is bounded to the supplied research's signup, minors, contract, email,
training, transfer, AI and platform topics. General tax, VAT, business registration,
sanctions screening, accessibility law, insurance, litigation posture and all
other local consumer or commercial rules are not a completed worldwide audit.
A country not named here is unassessed, not unrestricted. Current legal guidance
outside the confirmed research scope needs a fresh primary-source review.

## Distribution authority is unresolved

**DIST-01, all surfaces and plans.** No executable offered-country allowlist or
sanctions matrix was found in the scoped reads of web proxy/config/lib, compliance
contracts and current compliance documents. The Terms' export-control section is
policy text, not proof of screening. Provider residence, billing currency, catalog
membership and admission by a web function are not offer authority. The owner
must establish one approved surface/plan/route/country matrix and identify its
canonical executable owner. This document does not manufacture one.

**DIST-02, EEA.** The brief reports production refusal since 2026-10-02. Source
shows a conditional web gate: [country set and
flag](../../apps/web/lib/eu-access.ts) lines 1-32 and 55-69, and [proxy
rewrite](../../apps/web/proxy.ts) lines 256-286. Only recognised true values enable
the flag. Missing/unknown country headers and non-set countries pass this
function; declared webhook/cron routes and `/region-unavailable` are exempt,
and proxy matchers omit other specific paths. The detected-country rewrite uses
HTTP 451. No production flag, header accuracy, alternate entry or live response
was inspected. The existing [trust-claim
record](trust-claims.json) also labels deployment configuration unknown.
Preserve the owner's refusal; obtain a dated deployment attestation and coverage
proof from the lead. There is no request to enable EEA service.

**DIST-03, UK, Switzerland and other territories.** GB and CH are outside that
web block set; this proves only the gate's behavior. Store territories, downloads,
Local/BYOK distribution, payment eligibility, upstream regional restrictions and
existing accounts remain unverified. For EEA legacy processing and UK offering,
blocking alone does not settle representation or other obligations: see
[FRESH-01](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#fresh-01), [EUUK-N096](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#euuk-n096) and [EUUK-34](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#euuk-34) onwards. Do not infer a blanket exemption.

## Cross-cutting owner and lead decisions

1. **AGE-01, keep rule unchanged.** Reconcile the brief's 18 minimum with current
   supervised-teen wording and separate majority/consent ages. Do not silently
   convert adult account ownership into an adults-only actual audience. Establish
   whether supervised teenagers are intended, supported and routed with verified
   protections; counsel assesses local capacity and audience tests. Source records
   [APAC-10](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#apac-10), [USAM-N111](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#usam-n111), [EUUK-34](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#euuk-34), [INDIA-12](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#india-12) identify why one checkbox is not a
   global conclusion. This document proposes no age-rule edit.
2. **TRAIN-01, owner hold.** Specific quota no-training review and generic Free
   may-train notice have different scopes (E06, [FRESH-06](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#fresh-06)). Decide the inventory,
   account consent and third-party terms that support each route's disclosure.
   Public pricing/data-use/signup replacement wording remains held. No source
   review bit proves all Free routes or private account settings.
3. **RES-01, lead schema request.** Record selected endpoint access region,
   storage region and inference scope separately, with primary URL, verification
   date and route/override scope. Expose a canonical consumer binding. Keep
   unknown routes unknown. Current NON_US must not become a Singapore-only or
   US-exclusion guarantee (E07, [FRESH-06](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#fresh-06)).
4. **MAIL-01, account draft only.** Minimum accurate addition: optional, unticked
   product updates/launch-news choice, account record and account withdrawal
   (E03-E04, [FRESH-05](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#fresh-05)). Wider tips/offers awaits the lead's label/version change.
   No sender, delivery or emailed unsubscribe promise is added. Lead must prove
   consent audience selection and accessible sender-side opt-out before sending.
5. **REP-01, owner/counsel and lead.** Review present/legacy EEA scope and UK
   offering/representation facts, then reconcile the current public Article 27
   inference. [FRESH-01](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#fresh-01) states the territorial tests and exceptions; refusal alone
   resolves none of the factual legacy/monitoring questions. Preserve current
   restrictions while decisions are pending.
6. **PROVIDER-01, reviewed contract scopes.** Provider consumer terms are
   references, not AGI's API agreement. [PROVIDER-08](../research/global-distribution-research-2026-10-05.md#provider-08)-`24` retain commercial,
   age/audience, personal-data and production-use limits; being adults-only does
   not cure every unpaid/trial contract. Verify selected provider/route terms and
   account exceptions through the existing registry owner before publishing claims
   or expanding eligibility. The old snapshot is not current pool approval.
7. **INDIA-OPS-01, conditional duties.** Determine whether AGI is an intermediary
   or e-commerce entity and which current/future notice, response and retention
