# docs/product

Durable product truth: what the product is, what it must do, and what it must
not do. Requirements, capabilities, terminology, platform support, and the
commercial model.

Implementation status does not belong here, it changes faster than this tier
should. Put it in `docs/work/`.

The September 21 baseline is owned by `definition.md`, `requirements.md`,
`suite.md`, `commercial.md`, and `experience-contract.md`. Together they define
one ecosystem with two continuity domains and one account-level commercial
policy; a surface must not create private copies of those contracts.

`competitive-guidance.md` owns the standing evidence and parity discipline.
Its 192-point checklist is a research inventory, not a second product backlog
and not permission to implement every competitor feature.
