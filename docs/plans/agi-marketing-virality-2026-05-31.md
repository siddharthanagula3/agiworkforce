## 1. Executive Summary

**The one growth strategy to bet on:** **founder-led "build-in-public" + niche-community seeding + an AI-powered
short-form-video engine, with virality engineered INTO the product (not bolted on).** Paid ads are dead for a
low-ARPU app (CPI $4–5 iOS, underwater) (FACT); **distribution is the moat** (FACT).

**Realistic odds of "going viral":** low and not the plan. Virality is _engineered, not lucky_ (FACT) — but most
"viral" cases ride an existing network (Threads on Instagram's base, Midjourney's Discord) (FACT). For a solo
bootstrapped founder the realistic target is **compounding** (500–2K followers Y1, first customers in 6–8 weeks of
real community work), not a lottery spike. (FACT/ESTIMATE)

**The honest counter-finding you must absorb:** **privacy alone does NOT convert** (FACT). Lead with a \*named pain

- a visible "aha"\* (offline AI works, fast, no login), not "privacy is a right."

## 2. How marketing works now (2026) — the shifts to exploit

- **Paid UA is underwater for low-ARPU apps** (CPI $4–5 iOS / $3–4 Android; blended CAC much higher after churn)
  (FACT). Don't fund paid installs at launch.
- **Organic + word-of-mouth + community drive durable installs**; founder-led + build-in-public compounds (FACT).
- **Build-in-public works, measurably:** founders using it report **73% faster time-to-first-customer, 2.3×
  trial→paid, 89% more likely to hit PMF in 6 months** (FACT). Real cases: Pieter Levels, Bannerbear ($50K→$630K
  ARR), Nomad List ($2.7M ARR, _zero_ traditional marketing), Chatbase ($7M from 16 X followers) (FACT).
- **Founder personal account = ~8× the engagement of a company page** (FACT) — _you_ are the channel, not "AGI."

## 4. How things go viral — the replicable mechanics

- **Engineered loops > bolt-on referrals** (FACT): the _core action_ must distribute the product — Loom
  (share-to-view), Midjourney (public Discord gen), Canva (shared designs). "Invite a friend for credits"
  underperforms. (FACT)
- **Match the loop to psychology:** social currency (makes you look good), identity, utility (FACT). For AGI:
  **identity ("I keep my AI private") + a shareable artifact.**
- **Most virality rides an existing network** (FACT) — you don't have one, so **compounding community + built-in
  loop**, not a spike, is the plan.
- **Viral coefficient >1.0 needs built-in sharing** (FACT); realistic K for niche tools 0.5–1.2 (FACT) — below 1.0
  you still need a steady content engine.

## 5. AI as your marketing team (the engine a solo founder runs)

- **One person can produce 20–50 short-form pieces/week with AI** (scripting + AI voice + auto-edit + repurpose),
  ~10–20× cheaper than traditional; **1 founder ≈ a 3–5 person content team** (FACT/ESTIMATE). Tools cited:
  Opus Clip, CapCut, Submagic, HeyGen, Synthesia.
- **The winning pattern: AI for production, human for strategy + authenticity + final review** (FACT).
- ⚠️ **Faceless/low-effort AI content gets penalized** (YouTube 2026 inauthentic-content policy) and **fails in
  trust-dependent niches like privacy/security** — **a real human face (you) converts better here.** (FACT) So:
  **founder-led, AI-assisted** — not faceless AI farms.
- **Engine:** 1 long founder video/week → AI-cut into ~15–30 Shorts/Reels → schedule → analyze weakest metric →
  iterate. Repurposing a single asset across platforms = 3–4× ROI (FACT).

## 6. Viral loops to build INTO the product (k-factor)

- **Shareable artifact:** let the _output_ of using AGI be shareable (a result, a "made privately with AGI" card)
  — sharing = the core action, not a feature. (FACT)
- **Privacy-as-identity share:** "my AI runs on _my_ phone" is an identity statement people share (social
  currency). (ESTIMATE, grounded in the social-currency finding.)
- **India WhatsApp loop:** one-tap WhatsApp share is India's dominant organic loop — but **forwarding spreads
  installs, not retention** (FACT); pair it with a sticky core loop (the memory/personalization habit).
- **Avoid race-to-zero referral bribes** (FACT). Target built-in K, instrument it, don't fake it.

## 7. Privacy messaging that converts (resolving the paradox)

- **Don't say "privacy is a right."** Privacy alone doesn't convert; fear-based messaging _backfires_ (privacy
  fatigue + reactance after ~3–4 exposures) (FACT).
- **Do:** name a concrete pain for a high-WTP segment, then show architectural proof + a lived "aha":
  - "Keep therapy/journal notes off servers" (mental health)
  - "Confidential case data never leaves your phone" (legal — **+180% WTP**) (FACT)
  - "Works offline, instantly, no login" (mass consumer — utility hook)
- **Onboarding aha = the conversion event:** show offline inference _working_ in session 1 (fast, no account).
  Demo beats assertion. Measure **D1 retention**, not downloads (70% churn in 24h, 90% by D30) (FACT).
- **Empowerment + transparency framing** ("you own what your device learns") > fear ("they're spying"). (FACT)

share loop; turn first 20 users into evangelists via white-glove onboarding (FACT).

## 9. Metrics to track

- **View → install → D1-retention → paid** (not vanity views). D1 retention is the north star (FACT).
- **Per-platform:** completion/rewatch % (Shorts/Reels), reply-velocity in first 30–60 min (Threads), upvote ratio

- **K-factor** (target >0.5 within 6mo, >1.0 to self-sustain) (FACT).

## 10. Pitfalls & what we couldn't verify

- **Survivorship bias warning (FACT):** the viral case studies are outliers; most creators sit at modest numbers.
  Plan for _compounding_, not a spike.
- **Killed claims (1–2):** over-claimed/hype tactics dropped in verification (e.g. "just go viral with AI video").
- **Unverified:** exact India conversion for _this_ product, and whether the privacy-identity share loop actually
  fires, are **ESTIMATES** — instrument and test, don't assume.
- **The deciding tension:** the financial report says ramen-profit is **gated on distribution**; this report says
  distribution = **founder-led organic + built-in loop + AI content engine**, and that **privacy must be sold as
  named-pain + aha, not as a slogan.** That's the whole game for a $0-budget launch.
