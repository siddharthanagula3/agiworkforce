## 0. The 5 rules that override every tactic (from verified findings)

1. **Distribution is the moat. Paid ads are dead for us** (CPI $4–5 iOS, underwater at India ARPU) → organic only.
2. **You are the channel** — founder-led build-in-public beats a brand account 8×; post as yourself.
3. **Virality is engineered, not luck** — bake sharing into the _core product action_, not a bolt-on referral.
4. **Privacy is NOT the hook** — privacy alone doesn't convert (the paradox). Lead with a **named pain + an
   offline "aha"**; privacy is the _tiebreaker_. Never "privacy is a right" (fear backfires).
5. **D1 retention is the north star**, not downloads (70% churn in 24h). A great onboarding "aha" > any campaign.

## 2. The AI content engine (1 founder → a 3–5-person content team)

You can realistically produce **20–50 short-form pieces/week** solo with AI. **Founder-led, AI-assisted — NOT
faceless** (faceless AI gets penalized and fails in trust niches like privacy; a real human face converts here).

**The atom → repurpose loop (weekly):**

1. **Record ONE 20–40 min founder video/week** (you on camera: build-in-public update, a use-case demo, an honest
   struggle). This is the "content atom."
2. **AI-cut into 15–30 vertical Shorts/Reels** (tools: Opus Clip / CapCut / Submagic for auto-cut + captions).
3. **Schedule across YouTube Shorts → Reels → ShareChat/Moj** (a single atom repurposed = 3–4× ROI).
4. **AI-draft the text posts** (Threads/Reddit) from the same atom — but _you_ edit for authenticity (AI tone
   gets detected + disengaged).
5. **Review weakest metric weekly** (completion %, 3-sec hold, D1) → iterate next atom.

**Founder time:** ~30–60 min/day on Threads/Reddit engagement + one recording block/week. The AI does production;
you do strategy, face, and final authenticity pass.

**Cost:** ~$300–500/mo of tools max (you can start near $0 with CapCut + manual).

## 3. Privacy messaging that converts (resolve the paradox)

Do NOT say "privacy is a right" or run fear ("they're spying"). **Name a concrete pain for a high-WTP segment +
show the offline "aha."**

- Mental health / journaling: _"Keep your journal + therapy notes off any server."_
- Legal (highest WTP, +180%): _"Confidential case notes that never leave your phone."_
- Mass consumer (utility hook): _"A real AI that works offline, instantly, no login, no account."_
- The **aha to show in every demo**: open the app on airplane mode → it answers → "that ran entirely on your
  phone." Demo beats assertion.

Onboarding = the conversion event: show offline inference _working_ in session 1. Measure **D1 retention**.

## 4. In-product viral loop (Claude can build this — Track 1/3)

Sharing must BE the product action, not a button bolted on.

- **Shareable artifact:** let a result/insight export as a clean "made privately on-device with AGI" card
  (image, 9:16 for Stories/Reels). Identity + social-currency = the two strongest share triggers.
- **WhatsApp one-tap share** (India's dominant loop) — but pair with a sticky core loop (the memory/
  personalization habit) since forwarding spreads installs, not retention.
- **Avoid race-to-zero referral bribes.** Target a built-in k-factor; instrument share→install.
- This is a product change → it belongs in the Phase-A/Track-3 build, flagged here so it's designed in, not after.

## 5. The 90-day launch playbook

**Weeks 1–4 (pre-launch, app still hardening):**

- Start the founder account on **Threads + YouTube/Reels + Reddit**. Post the journey (40% product/demos, 30%
  honest metrics, 20% the build story, 10% takes).
- Seed 3–5 niche communities on the **80/20 rule** (give value for 3–4 weeks before any ask).
- Stand up a **Discord/Telegram + WhatsApp Channel** for early users; open a **waitlist** with a privacy+utility
  hook (ties to the existing InviteCodeModal / ALPHATESTER path).
- Stand up the **AI content engine** (first atom → first 15 cuts).

**Weeks 5–8 (launch):**

- **Launch on Product Hunt + Reddit + BetaList** the same morning. PH = credibility spike; **Reddit = the durable
  installs** (weight Reddit). Respond to every comment in the first 6 hours.
- Pursue **Apple/Google featuring** — a polished listing + the on-device-AI / App-Intents story is exactly what
  editorial favors. (Submit featuring nomination to Apple ~2–3 months ahead — start the paperwork now.)
- **ASO**: optimize screenshots + first impression + retention signals (2026 ranking weights D1/D7 retention over
  keywords). Localize screenshots for India (25–30% lift).

**Weeks 9–12 (grow):**

- Partner with **5–10 micro-creators (10K–100K, 3–5× engagement at 1/10 cost)** in privacy/AI/India-regional.
- Double down on whichever platform shows the best **D1**; instrument the in-product share loop.
- White-glove your first 20 users into evangelists (their stories become content).

## 6. Metrics (track weekly)

- **Funnel:** view → install → **D1 retention** → paid (not vanity views). D1 is the north star.
- **Per-platform:** completion/rewatch % (Shorts/Reels), reply-velocity first 30–60 min (Threads), upvote ratio +
  80/20 discipline (Reddit).
- **k-factor:** target >0.5 within 6 mo, >1.0 to self-sustain.
- **CAC stays ~$0** (organic). Only consider paid once LTV > $15–20.
