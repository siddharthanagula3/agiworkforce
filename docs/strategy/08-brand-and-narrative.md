Status: Strategy analysis (not source-of-truth)
Owner: Founder
Last updated: 2026-06-27

This translates the strategy into how AGI should _sound_ and the story it tells. It is a starting brand system, not a finished one — refine it with real customer language once design partners are live. The voice is derived from the one asset competitors can't copy: an honest, privacy-first architecture that does what it says.

---

## 1. The core narrative (the story arc)

- **The villain:** the metered black box. A single foreign model lab that takes your data, locks you in, charges you on a meter you can't see, and could change the terms tomorrow.
- **The hero:** the organization (and the developer) who refuses to hand over their data or their autonomy to one vendor.
- **The transformation:** from "renting intelligence from a black box" to "owning your AI workspace — your data, your providers, your infrastructure, your rules."
- **The stakes:** in a world where AI touches every document and decision, sending all of it to one lab is a sovereignty, compliance, and dependence risk you can't walk back.

One-sentence narrative: **AGI is the AI workspace you actually own — multi-provider, local-first, and private by architecture, not by promise.**

---

## 2. Positioning statement

> For privacy-sensitive organizations and developers who refuse to lock their data into a single AI lab, AGI is the multi-surface AI workspace that runs locally, brings your own keys, and routes across every major provider — because trust boundaries are enforced in code, not in a marketing page.

---

## 3. Messaging pillars

1. **Private by architecture.** Local Mode never silently leaves your device. The boundary is enforced in code and provable with tests — not a policy you have to trust.
2. **Your providers, your choice.** 15 providers, four routing tiers, one catalog. No lock-in. The model lab works for you, not the other way around.
3. **No metered black box.** Bring your own keys at no markup, or run models locally. Pay for the software, not a meter you can't see.
4. **One workspace, every surface.** Desktop, web, mobile, CLI, IDE, browser — the same product, the right sync boundary for each.
5. **Honest by default.** We claim only what we've shipped. (This is already true in your code and trust pages — make it a brand promise.)

---

## 4. Voice & tone

AGI sounds like a **senior engineer who respects your intelligence**: precise, calm, technically credible, allergic to hype. Closer to a security vendor than a consumer AI app.

| Attribute              | Do                                                                      | Don't                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Precise**            | State exactly what is true and shipped                                  | Use vague superlatives ("revolutionary," "magical") |
| **Honest**             | Say "in progress" / "planned" when it is                                | Claim parity or features that aren't shipped        |
| **Technical-credible** | Reference the architecture (trust boundaries, providers, local runtime) | Hand-wave the "how"                                 |
| **Calm**               | Let the substance carry it                                              | Manufacture urgency or FOMO                         |
| **Respectful**         | Assume a smart reader                                                   | Over-explain or condescend                          |
| **Plain**              | Short sentences, concrete nouns                                         | Jargon-as-decoration, buzzword stacking             |

Three quick voice tests before anything ships: (1) Is every claim true and shipped? (2) Could a competitor say the exact same sentence? If yes, sharpen to what only AGI can say. (3) Would a skeptical CISO nod, or roll their eyes?

## 6. Words to use / avoid

**Use:** own, private, local-first, your data, no lock-in, your keys, transparent, provable, trust boundary, sovereign, multi-provider, enforced in code.

**Avoid:** "AGI" used to imply artificial general intelligence (your name is a brand, not a capability claim — never imply you're building superintelligence); "revolutionary," "game-changing," "magical," "unlimited" (when it isn't); "ChatGPT killer" (positions you as derivative); any unshipped capability stated in present tense.

**One naming caution:** the product brand "AGI" will be read by some as the literal term. Decide deliberately whether to lean into it (bold, risky, invites "you're not building AGI" critique) or to consistently frame it as a workspace brand. The narrative above assumes the latter — AGI as _the workspace_, not a superintelligence claim.

## 7. Boilerplate (the "about" paragraph)

> **AGI** is the multi-surface AI workspace built for people who want to own their AI, not rent it from a black box. It runs across desktop, web, mobile, CLI, IDE, and browser; it works with local models, your own provider keys at no markup, and routes across every major AI provider. Its trust boundaries — what stays local, what uses your keys, what touches managed cloud — are enforced in code and provable with tests. AGI is private by architecture, not by promise.

(Trim to one or two sentences for app-store and social bios. Keep the "private by architecture, not by promise" line — it's the differentiator in seven words.)

---

## 8. Channel notes

- **Developer channels (GitHub, HN, docs):** lead with architecture and honesty. Show the trust-boundary code. Developers reward substance and punish hype — your voice is already built for this.
- **Enterprise / regulated buyers:** lead with sovereignty, compliance, deployment, and audit. Speak to CISOs and compliance officers, not end users. ROI = risk reduction + no lock-in, not "productivity magic."
- **App stores:** factual, scoped to shipped features (per `03` — no overclaiming; review teams penalize it).
- **Social / launch posts:** one true, specific, surprising fact per post (e.g., "Most AI apps are thin clients to a cloud. AGI runs a real model on your phone."). Avoid thread-bait hype.

---

## 9. Why this voice is a moat

Every other AI app sounds the same: breathless, superlative, hype-saturated. A calm, precise, honest voice that _demonstrably tells the truth_ (because the code backs it) is differentiated and self-reinforcing — it builds exactly the trust that privacy-sensitive buyers are buying. Your honest engineering culture (`03`) and your brand voice should be the same thing expressed in code and in words. Don't let marketing outrun the product; that's the one way to lose the only moat that's hard to copy.
