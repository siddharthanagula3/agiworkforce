## What these checks were used for

- **Provider-training setting (`WEB-PROVIDER-TRAINING-SETTING-01`).** OpenRouter
  treats a request without `data_collection: "deny"` as allowing providers that
  may train. The main chat path already sends deny for an OpenRouter router;
  background calls and AGI Code did not. The first fix makes deny the managed
  default. Video generation cannot carry the field, so it is refused for an
  account with the setting on.
- **Paid pricing cell.** The leader prints the default and the control per plan
  and no unconditional "No" where training depends on a setting.
- **Connector detail pages.** The leader publishes one indexable page per
  connector. AGI's default is stricter: the pages are built with `noindex` and
  stay out of the sitemap until metadata completeness has been measured.
- **Support entry point and voice wording.** The leader opens help from the
  account menu ("Get help") and names the two speech features "voice mode" and
  "dictation". These back the defaults of `L15-D1` and `L15-D6`.
- **Subprocessors wording for Apple.** "Nothing is sent back to Apple" is not
  accurate while online checks are enabled. "No purchase identifiers or
  personal data are sent to Apple" is.

These are product and engineering references. They are not a legal conclusion
about any disclosure.
